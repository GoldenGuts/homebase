# What it shows

Hours of screen time today (ring), this week, and per screen: TV, phone, computer and gaming PC.

# What it needs

Home Assistant already knows when each screen is on; history_stats turns that into hours. You need:

The phone's "interactive" sensor from the Home Assistant app (Settings → Companion app → Manage sensors → Interactive), a TV media player, and a sensor that is on while your computer is in use (the Home Assistant app for macOS, HASS.Agent on Windows, or anything that reports activity).

Replace the three placeholder entity ids in the package (binary_sensor.PHONE_interactive, media_player.TV, binary_sensor.COMPUTER_active) with yours.
